Who we are

Keynsham Seals is a swimming club for all ages and the majority of disabilities. It was set up in 1976 so that adults and children with disabilities and neurodivergence could learn to swim somewhere comfortable and welcoming, and it has been doing that ever since.

There are two hoists to help people into the pool, and full-access changing rooms. We are a friendly and supportive club: families look out for each other, and new members are made to feel at home from their first Sunday.

What Seals means to our families
